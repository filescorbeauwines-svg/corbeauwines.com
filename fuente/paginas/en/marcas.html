We build brands to carry our own risk

A company that only produces for others never sees what happens after the sale. It does not see the shelf, the reorder, or whether the consumer came back.

In our brands we put up the capital and take the consequences. What we learn there is what we bring to somebody else’s brand.

It is the most honest form of market research: the kind paid for with your own money.
