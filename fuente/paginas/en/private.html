Discretion

The brand is yours. The consumer is yours. We stay behind.

Backwards from the price

We start with the number, not with the wine

A private label project has a shelf price, a channel margin, a logistics cost, a tax and a tariff. What is left is the wine that can be made.

First we close the economics. Then we design the product that fits inside them.

The other way round, you end up with an excellent sample nobody can sell.

Starting point

The price your wine has to reach on the shelf

  • −

    Retailer margin

    It changes with the channel: a supermarket chain does not ask for the same as a wine shop or an online store.

  • −

    Importer or distributor margin

    It depends how many steps sit between the winery and the shelf. Sometimes one, sometimes two.

  • −

    Domestic taxes in the market

    Every country has its own, and they do not levy alike: some by value, others by volume or by alcohol content.

  • −

    Import tariff

    It depends on the trade agreement in force with Argentina in that destination.

  • −

    Logistics, insurance and clearance

    It varies with the format, the distance, the time of year and the exchange rate.

  • −

    Packaging: bottle, label, closure and case

    This is the part the project can move on purpose. Lightening a bottle frees cents that go back into the wine.

What is left is the wine that can be made

That number — the available cost per litre — is what defines variety, blend, ageing and oak. Not the other way round.

How much each component weighs changes with the market and with the project: there is no single proportion that fits all. What does not change is the list, and the order they are subtracted in.

After launch

The first order is the easy part

A private label is decided on repeat orders: the second production identical to the first, volume that can expand when rotation supports it, the wine still available when the chain adds it to another region.

That is where the control system matters. And where most private label projects break.
